About this restoration

This runs Vincent’s unchanged GAME and PQ calculator files in a custom TI-BASIC interpreter. The sprites, pictures, rules, menus, and quirks come from the original programs. Small text and automatic execution speed are approximations; this is not a full TI hardware emulator.

The 2014 save opens as a separate playable copy. Your current game, original backup, and any save from the previous website version are kept separate. Returning to the gameroom keeps calculator variables, just like stopping and relaunching a program.
